Notes.AI

Your personal AI note-taker. In any language in the room.

Notes.AI listens, transcribes and writes the notes for you — so you can be present in the conversation. It detects the language automatically and keeps an evidence-grade record: who said what, and when.

  • Auto-detects the language. Over 50 of the world's most spoken languages and the top 25 languages of India — including Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Bengali, Kannada and Punjabi — recognised as people speak.
  • Evidence-grade. A timestamped, speaker-labelled transcript you can rely on, stored in Kayak's own storage with a tamper-evident record.
  • Notes, not just a transcript. Summary, decisions, action items and open questions, written when the meeting ends.
  • Private by design. Processed on Kayak's own cloud platform, only when captions or notes are switched on, with a notice to everyone.

Built on Meet by Kayak's live captions and AI minutes, available today. Broader language auto-detection is rolling out.

Security & privacy

Confidential by default

  • Encrypted in transit, everywhere. Meeting audio and video travel directly between browsers, encrypted end to end by WebRTC; a relay only ever forwards encrypted media it cannot read.
  • Two-factor for everyone. Staff through Kayak Internal; clients with an authenticator app on every sign-in. Codes can't be reused; links expire.
  • Walls between clients. Each client sees only their own conversations and the people in them.
  • Audited & retained to law. Every sign-in and change is logged; security records kept 180 days per India's CERT-In Directions.
  • Built to Indian, EU & UAE privacy law. Notice and consent, data downloads, self-service account closure, a named privacy contact.
  • Certification roadmap. Working towards ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 Type II.

Questions

Is there a mobile app?

There's no separate app to download from an app store. Instead, open meet.kayaksa.com in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen — it installs as a mobile web app and gives you the full experience: meetings, chat, tasks, To-Do, Planner and notifications. On a computer, there are also native Windows and Mac apps.

Who can use Meet by Kayak?

Kayak employees, and the clients and partners a Kayak employee invites. Anyone can join a single meeting as a guest from an invitation link.

What is Notes.AI?

Kayak's AI note-taker. It transcribes the meeting, auto-detecting the language across 75+ world and Indian languages, and writes the summary, decisions and action items — kept as an evidence-grade, timestamped record. It builds on the live captions and AI minutes available today; wider auto-detection is rolling out.

I'm a client. How do I get access?

Your Kayak contact invites you by email. Open the link, accept the privacy notice and set up an authenticator app such as Microsoft or Google Authenticator. After that, sign in with your email and a 6-digit code.

Is a meeting recorded or transcribed?

Only if the host turns recording or captions on — and everyone sees a notice when they do. Recordings and transcripts are stored in Kayak's own storage.

How do I get help?

Write to enquiries@kayaksa.com. For privacy requests, put "Privacy" in the subject line; we reply within 30 days.
