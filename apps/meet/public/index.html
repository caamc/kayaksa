Where Meet by Kayak goes further

Designed for confidential advisory work

Privacy walls between clients

A client sees only the conversations they were added to and only the people in them. They cannot browse a directory, find other clients or start channels. Conversations with clients are marked External so no one forgets who is listening.

Two-factor for everyone, always

Kayak staff sign in through Kayak Internal (password and authenticator); clients use an authenticator app on every sign-in. Codes cannot be reused, and one-time links expire.

AI that stays on Kayak's platform

Captions, translation and recaps run on Kayak's own cloud account, only when someone asks for them. Your content is never used to train AI models and never shown to advertisers.

Your data rights, self-service

Anyone can download a copy of their data, and clients can close their account themselves: built for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR and the UAE's PDPL.

Mute from anywhere

The desktop app mutes you with one shortcut from any app, floats a mini window while you present (hidden from the people watching), and puts meeting controls in the Windows taskbar.

Calm by design

No ads, no upsells, no feeds. Chat notifications wait while you are in a meeting. Unread counts, @mentions and reminders are the only interruptions.

Security and privacy

Confidential by default

  • Encrypted in transit, everywhere. Meeting audio and video travel directly between participants' browsers, encrypted end to end by WebRTC. When a relay is needed, it forwards encrypted media it cannot read.
  • Signed, expiring links. Invitation and sign-in links are signed, single-use where it matters, and expire.
  • Every sign-in and change is logged with security records kept for 180 days, as India's CERT-In Directions require.
  • Files are shared only with the conversation, downloaded under a strict sandbox, and programs are refused.
  • Built to Indian, EU and UAE privacy law: notice and consent at sign-up, data downloads, account closure and a named privacy contact.
  • Certification roadmap: Kayak is working towards ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 Type II for its platforms.

Questions

Who can use Meet by Kayak?

Kayak employees, and clients and partners a Kayak employee invites. Anyone can join a single meeting as a guest from an invitation link.

I'm a client. How do I get access?

Your Kayak contact invites you by email. Open the link, read and accept the privacy notice, and set up an authenticator app such as Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator. From then on, sign in with your email and the 6-digit code.

I lost my phone. How do I sign in?

Ask your Kayak contact to reset your access. They send you a new one-time link to set up your authenticator again. For your security, no one at Kayak can see or recover your codes.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Meet by Kayak works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on computers and phones. The Windows and Mac app adds global mute shortcuts, a mini window and taskbar controls.

Is the meeting recorded or transcribed?

Only if the host turns recording or captions on, and everyone sees a notice when they do. Recordings and transcripts are stored in Kayak's own storage.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to enquiries@kayaksa.com. For privacy requests, put "Privacy" in the subject line; we reply within 30 days.
