Meet by Kayak for Windows and Mac

Everything in Meet by Kayak, plus what a browser tab can't do: mute yourself from any app, a mini window that floats while you present and stays out of what you share, meeting controls in the Windows taskbar, and unread counts on your dock or taskbar.

Made for the way advisers work

Global mute

Press Ctrl Shift M (⌘ ⇧ M on a Mac) from any app. Every shortcut can be changed.

Present without the clutter

When you share, the app shrinks to a floating mini window that the people watching never see.

Pick exactly what to share

A clear picker for a whole screen or a single window, with computer sound on Windows.

Taskbar and menu-bar controls

Mute, camera, share and leave from the Windows taskbar preview, the tray or the Mac menu bar.

Quiet while you meet

Chat notifications wait until your meeting ends. Reminders arrive before the next one.

Built for corporate networks

System, direct or manual proxy, a data saver for slow links, and settings IT can manage on every machine.

System requirements and installation

Windows

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (Intel, AMD or ARM)
  • Installs for you alone, no administrator rights needed
  • MSI for IT teams deploying with Intune or Group Policy: download the MSI
  • ARM laptops: download for ARM64

macOS

  • macOS 11 Big Sur or later
  • One app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs
  • Open the download and drag Meet by Kayak into Applications
  • The first time you share your screen, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording

Phone and tablet

  • There's no app-store app: Meet by Kayak runs as a mobile web app with every feature — meetings, chat, To-Do, Planner and notifications
  • iPhone and iPad: open meet.kayaksa.com in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
  • Android: open it in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Install app
  • It opens full-screen from your home screen, like any other app

For IT administrators

  • Lock any setting with a policy file: %ProgramData%\Kayak\Meet\policy.json on Windows, /Library/Application Support/Kayak/Meet/policy.json on macOS
  • Updates come only from meet.kayaksa.com and never install during a meeting
  • No telemetry or crash uploads
